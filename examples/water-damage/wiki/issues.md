# Issues and evidence

**C-1042 · Revision `r3`**

## How did the water reach the kitchen ceiling?

The insured suspected water entered through the roof after heavy rain. They reported seeing the wet patch and could not say where the water first entered.[^D-01-v1-p1]

The engineer considered a leak at the utility-room supply coupling one possible explanation. The visual inspection did not establish the source. Contractor A’s work order documents a repair at that coupling on 5 May. Taken together, these records leave the cause open.[^D-03-v1-p3-opinion][^D-02-v1-p1]

The engineer recommended a pressure test to distinguish between possible routes of entry.[^D-03-v1-p3-test] [Open action](action-plan.md) · [Claim index](index.md)
