# Engineering report

**D-03-v1 · Inspecting engineer’s report.** Received 18 May. [Receipt record](../../sources/D-03-v1.md#file-record)

The engineer recorded an inspection on 12 May of the kitchen ceiling and the utility room above.[^D-03-v1-p2-inspection] A leak at the supply coupling was one possible explanation for the moisture. The engineer could not establish its source by visual inspection.[^D-03-v1-p3-opinion] The engineer recommended a pressure test.[^D-03-v1-p3-test]
