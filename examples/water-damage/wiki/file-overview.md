# File overview

**C-1042 · 18 May · Revision `r3`**

The insured noticed a wet patch on the kitchen ceiling on 7 May after heavy rain and suspected roof entry.[^D-01-v1-p1] Two days earlier, Contractor A had replaced the cold-water supply coupling in the utility room.[^D-02-v1-p1]

The engineer considered a coupling leak possible. The visual inspection left the source of the water unresolved.[^D-03-v1-p3-opinion] The engineer recommended a pressure test.[^D-03-v1-p3-test] The three records listed below contain no test result.
